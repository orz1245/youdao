★功能特性Features

「生词高亮助手」将你在有道词典积累的生词本无缝带到日常浏览的网页中，把每一次英文阅读都变成轻松温习单词的机会。Vocabulary Highlighter maps your Youdao Dictionary wordbook onto everyday web browsing, turning every English article into an opportunity to review words effortlessly.

✦ 网页生词自动高亮Auto Page Highlighting
打开网页自动扫描，高亮生词本中的每个词；支持单页应用与无限滚动内容实时动态监听。 Scans and marks your saved words on any web page; supports SPAs and dynamically loaded text in real time.
⚡ 变体智能匹配（精度优先）Smart Inflection Matching
大小写、复数、时态、所有格智能识别，宁缺毋滥，有效防止误伤其他日常词汇。 Matches case, plurals, verb tenses, and possessives with precision-first rules to prevent false positives.
💬 悬浮即查音标与释义Hover Definition Bubble
鼠标停留稍作等待（600ms）平滑弹出气泡，展示音标与精简释义；贴近视口边缘时智能防遮挡。 Hover for 600ms to open a clean definition bubble with phonetics; smart edge guards prevent clipping.
🔊 真人发音与语音选择Audio & Voice Options
悬浮自动朗读或点击气泡内喇叭发音；支持选择 Google 在线语音或本地系统英语语音。 Audio on hover or on-demand speaker click; choose between Google online voices and system voices.
➕ 两种随手添加方式Two Ways to Add Words
悬浮未收录词点击气泡加号，或直接在网页上划词选中文本一键加入有道生词本。 Save words by clicking "+" in the hover bubble, or simply select any word on the page to add it instantly.
🗑️ 悬浮删除与就地撤销Hover Delete & Undo
已收录生词气泡中可一键删除，添加后支持立即撤销；一处删除，整页该词高亮同步移除。 Trash button in bubble to delete words with instant undo; removing a word clears all its highlights on the page.
☁️ 有道云同步与自动同步Cloud Sync & Auto Sync
一键双向拉取有道生词本；后台定时 Alarms 每日自动拉取最新词库，免去手动操作。 One-click sync with your Youdao wordbook, plus background alarms for automatic daily synchronization.
🔒 本地优先与隐私安全Local-First & Private
生词缓存于浏览器本地存储，离线可查；不记录浏览历史，无任何远端数据埋点上报。 Words are stored locally on your device; no browsing tracking, no telemetry and no third-party uploads.

1快速上手Quick Start

  1. 点击浏览器工具栏的插件图标，在设置弹窗中点击「登录」，在跳转的有道官网完成账号登录。 Click the extension icon in your browser toolbar, then click Sign in in the popup to log in to Youdao.
  2. 登录成功后点击「同步」按钮，将你的有道生词本完整拉取到本地浏览器存储中。 After signing in, click the Sync button to pull and store your full Youdao wordbook locally.
  3. 打开任意英文网页或文章，生词本里的单词及其变体形态将自动高亮呈现。 Open any English web page: words from your wordbook and their inflected forms are highlighted automatically.
  4. 鼠标停在生词上稍候片刻（约 600ms），浮出音标与释义气泡；开启发音时触碰即读。 Rest the cursor on a word for a moment (~600ms) to open the definition bubble; audio plays automatically when enabled.
  5. 遇到未收录的新生词，鼠标悬浮点击「+」加号，或直接划选文字点击「加入生词本」即可随手收藏。 Spot an unfamiliar word? Hover and click "+", or select text on the page and click "Add to wordbook" to save it.

2生词高亮与变体匹配Highlighting & Word Forms

扩展会将生词本里的每一个单词与网页文字精准对照，命中处施加柔和高亮底色。为避免「生词本里存了原形，网页里因为变体却不高亮」的困扰，匹配引擎遵循「精度优先（Precision First）」原则，智能支持常见的词形变换： The extension matches every word in your wordbook against page text and applies a custom highlight. To avoid missing inflected words, the engine uses precision-first heuristics to cover common English word forms:

变体类型Form Type 匹配示例（生词本 → 网页命中）Example (Saved → Matched) 规则与说明Rule Description
大小写变化Case apple → Apple / APPLE 句首首字母大写、专有名词、全大写标题均能命中Matches sentence capitals, title case and ALL-CAPS
单复数 / 第三人称单数Plural / 3rd Person cat → cats；match → matches；run → runs 规则复数词尾（-s, -es, -ies）及动词单三形式Regular plural endings (-s, -es, -ies) and 3rd person verbs
动词时态（分词与过去式）Verb Tenses play → playing / played 现在分词（-ing）、过去式与过去分词（-ed）Present participles (-ing) and past tenses (-ed)
比较级与最高级Comparatives big → bigger / biggest 白名单形容词变体（-er, -est），防止误伤名词Whitelisted adjective forms to avoid false positives
所有格归一化Possessives patient's / patients' → patient 自动剥离名词所有格并准确提取词干进行高亮和取词Normalizes possessive apostrophes to match base stems
缩写、连字符与重音词Compounds & Accents don't、state-of-the-art、café 连字符复合词、常用缩写及带拉丁重音的词完整识别Hyphenated compounds, contractions and accented words
精度优先原则：错高亮比漏高亮更影响学习体验（会导致用户误认词义）。因此匹配引擎经过严格收敛，如 hard 不会误匹配为 hardly，use 不会误匹配为 user。动态加载的内容（如推特流、单页应用 SPA）会被自动监听并即时高亮，无需手动刷新页面。 Precision-First Philosophy: A false highlight hurts readability and learning. The engine strictly guards against stem collisions (e.g. hard never matches hardly, use never matches user). Dynamic page updates (SPAs, infinite scroll feeds) are automatically monitored and highlighted without refreshing.

3悬浮释义与发音Hover: Definitions & Audio

1. 已收录生词的悬浮体验1. Hovering on Saved Words

当开启「悬浮发音」时，鼠标一碰到高亮生词就会立即朗读发音，无需点击；把鼠标停在生词上稍作停留（约 600ms），即刻浮出轻量释义气泡，包含单词原形、音标、中文释义、发音喇叭及删除按钮。 When "Speak on hover" is enabled, touching a highlighted word speaks it immediately. Resting the cursor on the word for ~600ms opens a sleek bubble with phonetics, definitions, a speaker button and a delete action.

2. 未收录新词的悬浮体验2. Hovering on Unsaved Words

阅读中遇到未收录但想了解的英文单词，把鼠标悬浮在该单词上稍作停留，同样会弹出释义气泡并朗读发音。气泡右上角提供「+」加号按钮，点击即可一键加入生词本。 Hovering over any unsaved English word opens the same bubble with definition and pronunciation, plus an "+" add button to collect it into your wordbook on the spot.

防误触与边缘保护：只有鼠标真正静止停留在单词上方才会触发浮层，快速划过或停留在单词间隙不会误弹；靠近屏幕或视口边缘时会自动调整浮层朝向，鼠标离开后平滑淡出，不遮挡连续阅读。 Accuracy & Edge Guard: The bubble only appears when the cursor rests steadily over a word, ignoring quick sweeps and gaps. Viewport edge guards prevent overflowing, and the bubble fades out smoothly when you move away.

5发音与语音设置Audio & Voice Settings

  • 悬浮发音开关：在弹窗「功能」区可随时关闭。关闭后鼠标划过生词不再自动发音，保留气泡内喇叭点击发音。 Speak on hover toggle: Turn it off under Features in the popup if you prefer silence while skimming; you can still click the speaker in the bubble.
  • 智能语音选择：默认「自动（推荐）」会按「Google 在线英语语音 → 系统本地英语语音」的优先级智能挑选音质最好的发音人。 Smart voice selection: "Auto (recommended)" intelligently selects high-quality Google English online voices first, then falls back to local voices.
  • 手动试听确认：在下拉列表中选择美式英语（en-US）或英式英语（en-GB）嗓音后，点击试听按钮可即时朗读示例词确认效果。 Preview button: Pick a specific US or UK English voice from the dropdown and hit the speaker preview button to hear sample pronunciation.
系统英语语音补充：若语音下拉列表为空，表明系统尚未安装英语语音包。macOS 用户可在「系统设置 → 辅助功能 → 朗读内容 → 语音」添加英语语音；Windows 用户可在「设置 → 时间和语言 → 语音 → 添加语音」安装。 Installing System Voices: If the voice dropdown is empty, your OS lacks English voice packs. On macOS, add voices in System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice. On Windows, use Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices.

生词本数据来自有道词典官方服务，扩展通过安全的浏览器会话认证进行同步：Your vocabulary is sourced from Youdao Dictionary and synchronized securely through your browser session:

  1. 弹窗「词典」卡片点击「登录」，浏览器打开有道官方页面完成登录。Click Sign in in the Dictionary card to complete login on the official Youdao site.
  2. 登录成功后卡片显示有道图标与生词总数，点击「同步」按钮拉取完整词库。Once signed in, the card shows your wordbook total; click Sync to pull all entries.
  3. 同步完成后刷新或打开新网页，词库中的单词即会被自动高亮。Once synced, all words are automatically matched and highlighted on target pages.

自动同步（推荐开启）Automatic Daily Sync

开启「功能」区的「自动同步」后，后台服务会借助 Chrome Alarm 定时器每日自动静默拉取更新，你在手机或网页端收藏的新词第二天会自动高亮。关闭后仅在手动点「同步」时更新。Enabling Auto sync lets the background service automatically refresh your vocabulary daily via Chrome Alarms, keeping your wordbook continuously up to date.

登出安全保护Sign-Out Safety

点击卡片右上角的「登出」按钮即可退出登录。登出后已同步到本地的生词本完整保留，网页高亮继续生效，但为了账号安全会隐藏增删生词入口。Click Sign out anytime. Already synced words remain on this device and highlighting continues, while word editing is disabled for security.

8登录失效与自愈机制Session Expiration & Recovery

有道登录状态可能因 Cookie 过期或长时间未活动而失效。扩展具备完善的健康探测与自愈机制：Youdao cookies may expire over time. The extension includes proactive session monitoring and self-healing:

  • 在同步生词本或添加/删除单词时发现登录失效；Detected when syncing wordbooks or adding/deleting vocabulary;
  • 打开插件弹窗时检测到会话 Cookie 已过期。Detected upon opening the popup when session cookies have expired.
红色自愈横幅：检测到会话失效后，网页顶部会浮出显目的红色提示横幅，提供「重新登录」按钮。点击后跳转有道登录；登录完毕切回网页，扩展会自动重新校验登录态并拉取生词本，横幅自动平滑消失，无需反复操作。 Self-Healing Banner: When a session expires, a red top banner appears with a Sign in again button. Once you log in and switch back to your tab, the extension automatically re-checks authentication, re-syncs, and dismisses the banner.

？常见问题解答Frequently Asked Questions

问：为什么有的生词明明在生词本里，网页上却没有高亮？Q: Why is a word in my wordbook not highlighted on the page?
请依次检查：① 弹窗中「生词高亮」总开关是否开启；② 是否登录并成功点过「同步」（生词总数大于 0）；③ 该词是否为非常规不规则变体，若是可直接在有道生词本补充该形态。Check three things: ① The "Highlighting" toggle is turned on; ② You have signed in and clicked "Sync"; ③ The word is an irregular form the matcher missed (add that exact form in your Youdao wordbook).
问：鼠标停在生词上，为什么没有弹出释义气泡？Q: Why doesn't the definition bubble pop up when hovering?
气泡设计了约 600ms 的悬停防抖延时，需要将鼠标光标平稳停在单词字符正上方；快速扫过或停留在单词间的空白区域不会触发，以防影响正常阅读。A 600ms steady hover is required to prevent accidental triggers while reading. Ensure the pointer rests directly on the word rather than in margin gaps.
问：划词选中文本后，为什么没有出现「加入生词本」菜单？Q: Why doesn't the "Add to wordbook" pill appear when selecting text?
划词添加功能需要处于已登录状态；若选中的单词已经在生词本中，为避免重复操作也不会弹出。双击文字会优先选词并取消菜单，给双击操作留出容错。Text selection requires an active sign-in. If the selected word is already in your wordbook, the menu is omitted to avoid duplicate entries.
问：发音没有声音怎么排查？Q: How do I troubleshoot missing pronunciation audio?
首先确认弹窗中「悬浮发音」开关已打开；其次检查弹窗发音区的「语音」下拉列表是否为空（若为空说明系统缺少英语语音包，请参考第 5 节安装）。另外也可直接点击释义气泡内的喇叭按钮测试发音。Verify that "Speak on hover" is enabled and that the "Voice" dropdown is not empty. If empty, install English system voice packs as described in Section 5. You can also test with the speaker button in any bubble.
问：删除生词时提示失败是什么原因？Q: What causes word deletion to fail?
通常是因为有道账号的登录会话已失效。点击页面顶部出现的红色横幅中的「重新登录」，登录完毕后重新尝试即可。This typically indicates an expired Youdao session. Click "Sign in again" on the red warning banner, then retry deletion after signing in.
问：我的生词数据或浏览网页会被上传到第三方服务器吗？Q: Is my browsing history or wordbook uploaded to third-party servers?
绝不会。扩展严格采用本地优先（Local-First）架构，生词仅从有道官方拉取并保存在你自己的浏览器本地，没有任何第三方服务器中转、无埋点、无远程上报。Absolutely not. The extension uses a strict local-first architecture; data is fetched directly from Youdao to your device with zero telemetry and no third-party servers.

🔒隐私保护与权限声明Privacy & Security

  • 本地优先运行：生词本同步到浏览器本地存储后，所有页面高亮计算与释义匹配均在本地瞬间完成。Local-First Execution: Once synced, all word matching and highlighting runs completely within your local browser sandbox.
  • 不收集个人隐私：扩展不收集、不解析、不上报你的任何浏览网页内容、搜索记录或个人身份信息。Zero Data Collection: The extension does not read, log or upload your page content, search history, or personal identifiers.
  • 权限最小化：仅申请同步有道生词本、语音发音及本地持久化所需的必要权限。Minimal Permissions: Only permissions strictly necessary for dictionary sync, audio playback and local storage are requested.
  • Cookie 仅用于登录判断：扩展读取 youdao.com 的登录 Cookie 仅用于判断你当前是否有道在线，从而展示账号状态。Cookie 绝不会被提取、拼接或发送到任何第三方服务器。Cookies Used Strictly for Sign-In Detection: The extension checks youdao.com session cookies solely to determine if you are logged in; cookies are never extracted, saved, or transmitted elsewhere.
  • 第三方工具身份声明：本扩展为独立开发者开发的效率工具，与网易有道官方无关；「有道」字样仅用于客观指明生词本数据的来源服务。Independent Third-Party Status: This is an independent open-source productivity utility not affiliated with NetEase Youdao; "Youdao" is used solely to identify the wordbook source.
